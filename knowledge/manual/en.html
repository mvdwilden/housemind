Section 1Getting started

Start here

What is HouseMind?

HouseMind is an App in Home Assistant. It watches your house, learns how you live, and then takes small things off your hands.

  • Light. On when you come in and it is dark, off when nobody is there any more. Outside on when it gets dark, also when nobody is home.
  • Window coverings. Closed when it gets dark, open in the morning.
  • Heating and cooling. Warm when someone is there. Lower at night, when a window is open or when you are away.
  • Sleeping and getting up. Wake up calmly with light and warmth. See Sleeping and waking up.
  • Fresh air. The ventilation goes higher with damp or stuffy air, and a heads-up to air a room.
  • Energy. Hot water and appliances when power is cheap. See Energy.
  • Water and upkeep. A heads-up about water that keeps running, an empty battery, a filter, the waste or a boiler that does not heat. See Water and Maintenance and reminders.
  • Emergency. With smoke, carbon monoxide, gas or a leak, HouseMind steps in at once and warns you. If there is a risk of frost, it sets the heating to at least 8 °C.

HouseMind starts carefully. It learns per skill and per room, in three steps. See How HouseMind learns.

  1. ObserveHouseMind watches and learns. It does nothing yet.
  2. ProposeHouseMind asks first. You choose, with one tap.
  3. AutonomousHas it often gone well? Then HouseMind does it itself. You can always set it back.

Everything stays in your house, and it works without internet. Your automations stay on, until HouseMind asks whether it may take one over.

After installation

Setting up

After a new installation, and after Back to factory settings, you first see only this screen, also on the wall tablet.

This is what it looks like · the start screen

HouseMind is not set up yet

You set HouseMind up page by page: the introduction, the link with Home Assistant, Vesta, the AI, and done. Until then HouseMind does nothing of its own accord.

Emergency reactions, such as for smoke or a leak, already work.

Set up

Only an administrator sees the Set up button; others wait until it is done. The manual is already there, at the question mark at the top right.

Setting up is one row of pages, with for example "3 of 9" at the top and Back and Next at the bottom. If you say an answer out loud, Vesta goes on right away. If you stop halfway, you go on later where you were.

  1. IntroductionThe questions of the introduction. On Expert also Parts: how far HouseMind may go per part.
  2. About the houseAt which level new residents start, who lives here without their own phone (such as children), and whether Vesta may read the calendar.
  3. Link with Home AssistantTap Link and Yes, link; HouseMind does the rest. If it does not work yet, choose Yes, link later. See HouseMind in Home Assistant.
  4. Name and voiceChoose her name and voice. You read whether what you say to Vesta stays in the house or goes through the cloud. After that she can get an Assist pipeline of her own.
  5. AINot needed: without AI everything in the house works. With Next you skip this.
  6. DoneWait until the history has been read. If that stands still for a while, you can go on anyway. Here you also choose whether HouseMind fetches new knowledge by itself.
  • After Done HouseMind watches, with everything on Observe, and the Tour follows.
  • The language of the house is the language Home Assistant shows the administrator. See The language of the house.
  • A house that was already running counts as set up.

Vesta's own Assist pipeline

With an Assist pipeline of her own, you talk to Vesta through Assist, in her voice and in the language of the house. This works once the link is there: under Name and voice, or later under House settings › Vesta. Tap Yes, make the pipeline.

  • If you change her name, her voice or the language of the house, HouseMind adjusts the pipeline.
  • Ordinary commands, such as turning on a lamp, are handled by Home Assistant itself.
  • If Home Assistant already has a wake word with her name, the pipeline gets it. See "Hey Vesta".
  • If a pipeline already uses HouseMind's agent, Vesta asks whether she may take it over.
  • Your own pipelines stay as they are. Factory settings only remove her own pipeline.

The first time

Introduction

The first time you open HouseMind, Vesta asks at most four short questions, out loud if her voice is on. Choose with a tap and tap Next. Or tap Say it and say, for example, "level two" or "yes". With Later you skip it.

  1. Your levelFor everyone. You can always change it, under My preferences.
  2. Emergency notificationsOnly on Carefree: a notification on your phone with smoke, gas or a leak? On the other levels you always get them.
  3. May I learn?Only an administrator, once for the whole house. Without learning, HouseMind only reacts to emergencies.
  4. PetsOnly an administrator. While the house sleeps or nobody is home, what only a sensor sees in their rooms then does not count as a person.

With your level it also says: HouseMind helps with safety, but does not replace a certified smoke detector, CO detector or alarm system.

What happens next

After your yes to learning, HouseMind reads the history of Home Assistant; see Learning from the history. The first proposals come within a day. After a few weeks HouseMind does a lot itself.

Tour

After the introduction, Vesta shows you where everything is. Each time one part of the screen lights up, with a small card with her explanation. If her voice is on, she says it too.

  • You see what belongs to your level: on its own, at most 8 cards. The last card has Done.
  • It comes at a quiet moment, when nothing else is open, and never on the wall tablet. In an emergency it stops at once.
  • Once per person. If you choose a higher level, Vesta offers to show you what has been added.
  • One card, Help and tour, points out the question mark and the compass.
  • Again? Tap the compass at the top right, Tour.

The voice of the house

Vesta

Vesta is HouseMind's voice: the help in the house. She talks warmly and briefly, and never says the same thing twice in a row. When all is calm she sometimes makes a dry joke; in an emergency never.

Vesta decides nothing herself. She tells you what HouseMind did, what it wants to ask and why. An administrator can give her another name; see House settings › Vesta.

Vesta and your privacy

  • The microphone is only open while Vesta listens. Your voice goes to the speech recognition of Home Assistant. Sound is never kept; the conversation only as text.
  • With a local voice everything stays at home. With a cloud voice, only her sentence goes to that service.
  • An AI is not needed. If one is on, it only gets facts with neutral names, and it decides nothing.

Typing or talking

What you can ask Vesta

Vesta knows a lot about your house, also without AI. Ask it typed or out loud; you find examples under Ask something.

  • About the house: whether something is on, open or locked, how warm it is, what she did today, power and sun. Who is home she only says as a number, never who.
  • Commands: pause, continue, undo, pause the lighting, or the house mode. An administrator can also set a step.
  • Where something is. She tells you and asks "Shall I open it for you?". She never changes anything herself. She only opens House settings for an administrator.
  • What is waiting for you: "Do I need to do anything?", "Which house rules are there?" or "Is something broken in Home Assistant?".
  • How something works: from this manual, with Read more. Such a question does nothing: "How does pause work?" does not pause anything.
  • She never erases anything. With "forget everything" she points to House settings › Start again.
  • On a speaker or the wall tablet she does not know who you are. So you make a house rule in the app.
  • She doesn't know? With an AI she checks (via AI). Without AI she honestly says so, and points to the part of the manual that comes closest.

Is something waiting for your answer? Then Vesta asks you herself, in her window, from Informed. One question at a time:

  1. A quiet momentShe waits until no other screen is open and you are not typing.
  2. One questionIt stays, with its buttons, until you answer or close the window.
  3. You answerTap a button. Or tap Vesta and say it: she only listens after that tap.
  4. AfterwardsShe says what she does with your answer. Only then does the next question come.

How Vesta names things

Vesta names devices the way you do: "the lamp in the bedroom", not the name from Home Assistant. She leaves out names of people, numbers, postcodes and brands, also when an AI makes her words.

Only if two rooms would otherwise sound the same, such as two bedrooms, does she say the name that tells them apart. Lists in the app keep the name from Home Assistant.

With or without AI

  • To the AI go the last sentences of the conversation, your open notifications, the house rules and your own choices, with neutral names. The AI may also look things up in HouseMind, only reading, for questions like why was it cold in the bathroom yesterday.
  • Having something done? The AI only proposes it. Vesta asks you with buttons, and only does it after your yes, through the safety gate.
  • If the AI drops out (budget used up, not reachable, or no answer after 20 seconds), she goes on as without AI. In an emergency she never uses an AI.

Her voice

Vesta speaks out loud on the device where you tapped her, and on speakers in the house. Which voice that is, an administrator chooses under House settings › Vesta. She always speaks the language of her sentence; if the chosen voice cannot, she takes a voice that can.

A browser only plays sound after a first tap. So your first tap on Vesta turns the sound on.

The language of the house

If an administrator chooses another Language of the house, HouseMind adjusts her Assist pipeline after a yes. If her voice does not speak that language, she takes the recommended voice.

Your choices

Under My preferences › About me, at How does Vesta address you?, you choose between Casual and Formal. Under This device you choose Vesta aloud on this device; if this is off, you only see text. The speaker in her window does that with one tap.

Welcome home

When you come home, Vesta greets you briefly on the speaker or wall tablet in the first room where HouseMind sees you, and mentions what is going on. Also on Carefree, but not when the house is asleep.

On her own

Vesta speaks on her own in an emergency (on every speaker, also at night, with the fixed safety sentence), for the morning briefing and the bedtime check, and for something that needs you now, such as a fridge door that is open. Only where someone is, not too often, and not when the house is asleep; see When HouseMind speaks on its own.

In the app on your phone

Did you not hear Vesta in the room, but is HouseMind open on your phone? Then you get her sentence there, in your own language and at your own level; in an emergency everyone gets it. You only hear her voice when the app is in view and you have already tapped it.

Section 3How HouseMind learns

Step by step

How HouseMind learns

HouseMind learns per skill and per room. A skill is something HouseMind can do, such as "light when entering". In each room it goes separately through three steps: Observe (watching), Propose (asking first) and Autonomous (doing it itself).

  • The kind of day counts (workday, day off or holiday), and the time of day.
  • New habits win. After two weeks, something old only counts for half.
  • Fair counting. What HouseMind did itself does not count as "this is how you do it". What an automation does counts for half. Not now and no answer are not a no.
  • Everyone on Carefree? Then HouseMind asks nothing, but carefully tries something you can easily put back.
  • The limit. Under House settings › Learning and steps › Parts an administrator chooses how far HouseMind may go per part. Learning never goes beyond that. On Off, HouseMind does nothing with it and learns nothing; what it already learned is kept.

Who is there

HouseMind does not see who is there, only that someone is there: from motion and presence sensors, and from lamps and switches someone operates.

  • Only people count. An integration that only adjusts the colour of a lamp does not count. While a robot vacuum drives, motion counts nowhere.
  • Presence sensor. When it sees nobody any more, the room is empty at once. When it is offline, it does not count.
  • Only a motion sensor? It cannot see whether someone sits still. So after the last motion, Someone there stays for a few more minutes.
  • No sensor? A door or switch only says that someone was there. So HouseMind does not put the light out there by itself. If an administrator set that rule higher themselves, that choice stays saved; it applies as soon as there is a motion or presence sensor.
  • A toilet with only a door sensor? For a toilet or bathroom with one door and no other sensor, HouseMind asks once whether it is a small room with one door. If you say That's right at Brain › What I know, it watches the door. Closed after going in: someone is there. Opened and closed again: gone; the light first dims and then goes out. If the door is open, HouseMind does nothing.
  • Only inside. Someone in the garden, on the driveway or in the garage does not count. A person a camera inside sees does.
  • Pets. In rooms an administrator chooses, what only a sensor sees does not count as a person while the house sleeps or nobody is home – a presence sensor too, as it sees a dog just as well. No lamp goes on for it then, and the house does not wake up from it. A sign of a person does count: a camera that sees a person there, someone using a switch there, someone who just came from a room without pets (down the stairs, out of the bathroom), or a resident who just came home. From half an hour before your usual time to get up, movement wakes the house again. See House settings.

Dark enough, and the curtains

When is it dark enough for light? HouseMind learns that per room. When someone comes in while no lamp is on, it notes how high the sun is, what the light sensor measures, and whether a lamp went on.

  • The sun wins over a light sensor that also says "dark" during the day.
  • Every room its own light. Rooms that share one presence sensor, such as an open kitchen and dining room, are rooms of their own. Walk into the kitchen in the dark and its light goes on, also when the dining room is already lit. If you keep switching the kitchen light off then, HouseMind learns that the dining room's light is enough there. Switch it on yourself later, and the kitchen gets its own light again.
  • Walking through. Where you often walk through without light, the light only goes on after 10 seconds.
  • Curtains and shutters. HouseMind also learns per room when they close in the evening. In the bedroom they open in the morning when you are out of bed, nobody sleeps there any more and it is light outside. Without a sensor there, only when everyone is out, or at a time HouseMind learned from you.

Lighting

  • Coming in and leaving. In the dark the light goes on at once. If the room has been empty for a while, it dims for a moment and goes off; if you come back, it stays on.   • Light follows the day: brighter and whiter by day, softer and warmer in the evening. With Adaptive Lighting you set that up there; on Autonomous HouseMind takes it over for those lamps, until a step back. Without that integration HouseMind does it itself.
  • Worked it yourself? As long as someone is in the room, your choice stands, for a few hours at most. If you often choose another colour or brightness, HouseMind remembers it.
  • Outside in the evening the lamps go on when it gets dark, also when nobody is home, so the house looks lived in. They go off when the house goes to sleep, at the latest at your usual time (23:30 at first). When nobody is home, around bedtime, a bit differently each evening.
  • Coming home in the dark: the lamps at the entrance, such as on the driveway, go on and off again after 5 minutes, or after half an hour at most if someone is still outside. A garden or terrace at the back stays dark. If you switch the lamp on again afterwards, it stays on longer from then on.

  • Not: switching outdoor lights off because nobody is there, colours or flashing, or the indicator light of a device. In a garage, light when entering only if an automation already did that.

Section 7Safe and private

Safety

What HouseMind never does

Never, not even in an emergency

  • Turn off an alarm.
  • Open a lock.
  • Open a garage door, gate or door, not even behind a switch or in a group.

This is fixed inside HouseMind itself. No setting, update or AI makes it looser.

What it does in an emergency

  • Smoke or carbon monoxide: all lights fully on and white, so you can see the way out. With smoke, the ventilation off.
  • Gas: the gas valve closes, and nothing else, to prevent sparks.
  • Leak: the main water valve closes.
  • Frost is not an emergency: an Important notification, and the heating in that room to at least 8 °C.

A valve only closes if it is in Home Assistant. Emergency reactions always work: also during a pause, without learning, and when the steps are limited.

Furthermore, HouseMind leaves alone a device that was just operated by hand, or that another system or an automation controls. A device that drops off the network for a moment and comes back does not count as operated by hand. It never makes, changes or deletes automations; it only turns one off if an administrator wants that. It only closes a main water valve. It never turns on an oven or hob, and only turns one off after your yes. It never speaks through a TV, and a moment never operates a garage door or gate. See also Never with energy.

An alarm outside

Does a sensor outside report smoke, carbon monoxide or gas, such as a camera on the terrace that hears a smoke alarm? Then everyone gets an Emergency notification, also at night, and Vesta is on Attention. Nothing else: no lights, no speaking. It may come from the neighbours. Water and frost outside are just the weather; HouseMind does nothing with them.

Outside is a room called, for example, garden, terrace, balcony, driveway or veranda.

How the safety gate works

Everything HouseMind wants to do goes through the safety gate first: also an idea from the AI, a house rule and your "Do it". The gate asks six questions, always in the same order.

For Expert

With smoke, carbon monoxide or gas, HouseMind switches nothing else until the last detector is quiet again. If a detector stops responding after an alarm, HouseMind carries on after two hours, with a notification. A rain, soil or plant sensor is not a leak, and a freezer does not count for frost.

HouseMind helps with safety, but it does not replace a certified smoke detector, CO detector or alarm system. Keep those in the house and test them regularly. You use HouseMind at your own risk.

Stop using HouseMind

Do you want to stop? Then give everything back first, so HouseMind no longer does anything itself. Only an administrator can do this.

  1. OpenHouse settings › Start again › Removing HouseMind.
  2. Give backTap Give everything back and then Yes.
  3. Only watchingAutomations that HouseMind turned off are back on, everything is on Observe, and the link in Home Assistant is gone. Emergency reactions keep working until you remove it.
  4. RemoveIn Home Assistant: Settings › Apps › HouseMind.

Changed your mind? Tap Turn it back on. Rather start again? See Start again.
