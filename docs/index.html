HouseMind

A house that thinks for itself.

Add to Home Assistant

HouseMind is a Home Assistant App that learns how the people in your house live and looks after light, curtains, heating, water and energy for you – calm, explainable and safe. No automations to build.

What it does

  • It starts by watching. After your "yes" it learns, first proposes and only acts on its own once you agreed often enough.
  • Every action has a reason in plain words and can be undone with one tap.
  • Smoke, CO, gas and leaks get an answer from minute one.
  • It plans energy honestly – and shows what it saved, also when it turns out worse.
  • It never turns off an alarm, never opens a lock, a garage door, a gate or a door.

Four levels – you choose

Carefree
HouseMind takes care of everything; you hardly notice it.
Informed
You see what happens and why.
Involved
You approve proposals and set house rules.
Expert
You see and control everything, in detail.

Your house stays yours

Everything stays in your house. AI is optional and only ever proposes; names, addresses, images and history never go to a cloud AI. No telemetry.

Privacy statement

HouseMind runs entirely in your own Home Assistant. What it learns and keeps stays in your house. Something only goes out when an administrator says yes to it, or when you want a message on your phone.

What HouseMind keeps, and for how long

  • What happens in the house: lights, curtains, heating, who is home, and what HouseMind did and why. This is kept for 30 days. After that only summaries remain, such as learned habits and daily totals.
  • Your conversations with HouseMind, typed or through the voice assistant, as text. Never recordings. Also 30 days.
  • What HouseMind learned, your house rules, moments, settings and preferences: until you remove them.
  • Messages and proposals: an information message leaves the list after a week, settled messages are deleted after 30 days and proposals after 90 days. Weekly reports and energy totals are kept for 2 years.
  • A key for an AI service is stored encrypted in the App's own storage. What unlocks it is kept there too: whoever has that storage or a backup can read it. It never appears in a log or in the diagnostics package.

Everything is in the App's own storage in Home Assistant and is part of Home Assistant's backups.

What goes out, and why

  • By default only the knowledge updates below; nothing about your house goes along. Otherwise HouseMind works without internet.
  • AI, only when an administrator turns it on and gives consent: a short question with neutral names, such as 'lamp 3' or 'bedroom 2'. To understand your home also a device's kind, make and model, what the integration itself calls an entity (such as 'Child Lock'; never a name you gave), and a room's name without people's names. For a question to Vesta, the AI may also look up how a room is, what HouseMind did and why, and a summary of at most 7 days, also with neutral names. Never names of people, your address, your location, calendar contents or sound. Only the AI of Home Assistant in the house may describe a camera image, if an administrator turns this on; the image stays in the house. The chosen AI service handles the question under its own terms. At the Expert level you can see every question that was sent.
  • Forwarding in the voice assistant, only with consent: a question HouseMind cannot answer itself goes to the AI or assistant the administrator chose.
  • Knowledge updates: HouseMind fetches new knowledge about device types, countries and languages. Nothing about your house goes along. An administrator can turn this off.
  • Messages on your phone go through Home Assistant's notifications. On the way, the text passes through your phone's notification service.
  • Speech: what you say to Vesta and what she says goes through Home Assistant's voice assistant. If that uses a cloud service, your voice goes to that service; HouseMind tells you when it does. With Piper, Whisper or another local speech service everything stays in the house.
  • The calendar: HouseMind reads Home Assistant's calendar only after an administrator's yes, for the waste reminder, the morning briefing and days that are different. What is in it stays in the house.
  • The diagnostics package only goes out when you share it yourself. Names and devices in it are replaced by neutral labels.

Who sees what

  • Every resident chooses their own level. The higher the level, the more detail, such as the decision trace at the Expert level.
  • House settings, AI and the diagnostics package are only for Home Assistant administrators.
  • HouseMind does not watch or listen through cameras or microphones. Only if an administrator turns this on, and the AI of Home Assistant works in the house, does that AI describe what can be seen on the image, with a message; the image stays in the house.
  • Guests, babysitters and other people in the house: tell them. HouseMind sees movement and presence per room, no names; guest mode takes them into account. Do not point cameras at the street or the neighbours: HouseMind never keeps images, but the camera does record them.

What you can do yourself

  • See what HouseMind did and why: tap Why? at an action.
  • Put something back: tap Undo, or just put it back yourself.
  • Turn learning off in House settings. Then HouseMind only reacts to emergencies and sends messages.
  • Give everything back in House settings: HouseMind switches the automations it took over back on, and goes back to only watching.
  • Erase everything: House settings → Start again → Back to factory settings. Or remove the App in Home Assistant. Then everything HouseMind kept is gone.

Questions about privacy? Ask them on HouseMind's project page.

Get started in three steps

  1. Press "Add to Home Assistant" above and confirm.
  2. Install HouseMind from the App store in Home Assistant and start it.
  3. Open HouseMind in the sidebar. Try Demo mode first if you like – your real house is not touched.
Architecture
One brain with a world view, skills and learning in steps (observe → propose → act). Every action – from a skill, a house rule, AI, voice or Home Assistant – passes the same safety gate with hard limits in code.
Knowledge is data
Device kinds, countries and languages are YAML files in knowledge/. A new language or device kind is a file, not code. Signed knowledge bundles can update them between releases.
Home Assistant integration
A small integration shows HouseMind's state as entities, offers actions (pause, house mode, feedback, house rule, ask) and is a conversation agent for Assist. It talks to the App over the Supervisor network only.
Contributing
Translations and device kinds are the easiest way to help. Code changes need green tests: pytest, ruff, mypy and the frontend checks. See CONTRIBUTING.md.
